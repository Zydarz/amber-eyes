**[SFX]:** The rapid clatter of computer keys, followed by the rhythmic hum of a laser printer in an active squad room.

Through Anacrim, we systematically linked homicides that had previously appeared completely disconnected, including Martine Matias and Mireille Normand.
Furthermore, this rigorous cross-collaboration with forensic psychiatrists established the blueprint for France's Behavioral Analysis Unit, the GAC.
For the first time in French legal history, behavioral profiling was formally recognized as an indispensable, scientific pillar of criminal investigation.
We no longer confined our search to latent fingerprints and blood spatter; we began decoding the invisible psychological imprint left by the perpetrator.
