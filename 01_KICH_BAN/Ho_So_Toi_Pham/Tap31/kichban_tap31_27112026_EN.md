**INVESTIGATOR** *(presenting the timeline from 2020)*:
"According to initial investigative findings released by the Lang Son Provincial Police, Doan Van Sang and Mr. Nguyen Xuan Dat became acquainted via social media around 2020.

**[Pause 1.2 seconds – transitioning to Victimology analysis]**

**PSYCHOLOGIST** *(analyzing Victimology)*:
"In behavioral science, when analyzing a case, we never blame the victim; instead, we examine the structure of vulnerability known as Victimology.
However, in this specific file, there is a deeply strange and frightening psychological paradox that we cannot avoid: it was the victim himself who actively sought out and made extreme proposals to multiple different individuals in cyberspace.
Many people who received that proposal refused or walked away, but Doan Van Sang was the only one who agreed to follow through.
Through the lens of criminal psychology and forensic psychiatry, the phenomenon of a victim voluntarily seeking someone to carry out an extreme act upon themselves typically operates through four deep layers of mechanisms.
First is social alienation and an identity void: a young person who leaves home and loses connection with family and community for years falls into a state of chronic isolation.
When the anchors of real life are severed, their sense of self-worth erodes until they can no longer feel the meaning of ordinary existence.
Second is self-destruction through another person: in certain individuals carrying severe psychological trauma or drawn into extreme deviant scripts, the need to be completely dominated and the need for self-destruction can merge.
Repeating the proposal to multiple people shows this was not a fleeting impulse, but an obsessive script nurtured persistently in the mind until natural self-preservation instincts were completely paralyzed.
Third is the echo chamber effect of anonymous groups: in the real world, if an abnormal thought is voiced, family and society immediately push back to pull that person back onto a healthy track.
But inside the closed echo chamber of social media, those deviant thoughts are never checked; instead, they are encouraged, repeated, and gradually normalized.
And fourth — the most chilling point — is the pathological lock-and-key phenomenon between two deviant minds.
Numerous other people, when confronted with the victim's proposal, stopped because their moral boundaries and fear of the law remained intact.
Only when that proposal reached Doan Van Sang — someone already harboring power fantasies and cognitive distortion — did the two deviant halves lock together like a lock and key.
The perpetrator could even delude himself into believing he was merely fulfilling an existing request, thereby erasing any sense of guilt.
Yet, under the lens of criminal law and human ethics, human life is inviolable and cannot be bargained away.
Regardless of who initiated the proposal, a victim's consent never legalizes or mitigates the act of taking a life."

**HOST** *(raising the question of the escalation trajectory)*:
"Five years is a very long time.
From the very first messages in 2020 to the fateful appointment in early 2025, what was happening inside the mind of the man behind the screen?"

**PSYCHOLOGIST** *(decoding the Fantasy Addiction Cycle & Escalation Trajectory)*:
"Criminal psychology calls this process the Fantasy Addiction Cycle and the Escalation Trajectory, operating on the mechanism of stimulus tolerance.
Initially, deviant conversations online provide a certain dose of psychological stimulation, enough to satisfy abnormal emotional needs for a period of time.
However, just like any other addiction mechanism, the brain gradually adapts to the old dosage and falls into a state known as fantasy hunger.
Words on a screen, images, or anonymous discussions slowly grow dull and can no longer deliver the same sense of power or gratification as before.
To recapture that intensity of stimulation, the person nurturing the fantasy is forced to continually raise their own threshold.
They begin feeling the urge to drag the virtual world into reality, testing every small moral boundary until they cross a line of no return.
That is why extreme acts are rarely a sudden, spontaneous outburst, but rather the culmination of a fantasy-nurturing process spanning many years."

It is tied to a need to assert absolute control and an urge to record, preserve, or display an imprint within the underground world where they used to interact.
This very contradiction between the instinct to conceal in real life and the deviant desire to exist in the virtual world often creates fatal loopholes.

How can a man in his fifties walk out of a crime scene, wash his hands, and return to sit at his desk as if nothing had ever happened?"

**PSYCHOLOGIST** *(dissecting Perspective on the Victim — Objectification & Dehumanization)*:
"For a human being to cross the most fundamental moral barrier of taking another's life, their brain must undergo a process called Dehumanization and Objectification.
In a normal psychological state, when we see another human being, we activate our emotional empathy network: we recognize they have pain, a family, a past, and a future.
We understand the mental state of the person in front of us through a capacity called Theory of Mind.
However, within psychological profiles characterized by severe deficits in emotional empathy, the ability to resonate with another's pain is completely disconnected.
The victim is no longer perceived as a full human being with sentience and dignity.
Through years of interaction in deviant groups, the victim is gradually downgraded in the perpetrator's perception into a test subject, a prop, or a tool to satisfy control fantasies.
Once the victim has been stripped of personhood in the perpetrator's mind, feelings of pity or remorse no longer arise.
Mr. Nguyen Xuan Dat's social isolation after leaving his hometown for years only made it easier for the perpetrator to delude himself that no one would search, and no one would speak up."

**PSYCHOLOGIST** *(dissecting Compartmentalization)*:

In the second compartment is the dark world of closed groups, where all moral standards are swept aside to serve deviant desires.
Compartmentalization allows them to slam the steel door shut between these two compartments: the moment they step away from the scene, they immediately return to the first compartment without letting guilt leak into daily life.
Family and work are no longer moral anchors preventing them from wrongdoing; instead, they inadvertently become a normalizing shield.
They use the very ordinariness of family dinners, office roll calls, and regular social media interactions to reassure themselves.
In their mind, as long as the first compartment runs smoothly, the second compartment might as well not exist in the eyes of the world."

**INVESTIGATOR** *(connecting to the real-life tragedy of relatives and colleagues)*:
"In investigating many similar cases, we call innocent relatives and colleagues the secondary victims of the tragedy.

**HOST** *(transitioning to self-justification)*:

**PSYCHOLOGIST** *(analyzing Self-Justification & Moral Neutralization)*:
"Running parallel to compartmentalization is the Self-Justification system, also known as Techniques of Moral Neutralization and Moral Disengagement.
Human beings rarely view themselves as villains; even those who commit the gravest acts tend to build hidden rationalizations to lull their conscience to sleep.
In the context of deviant online groups, one of the most common neutralization mechanisms is distorted rationalization or blaming circumstances.
The offender may convince themselves that the other party voluntarily entered the conversation and voluntarily traveled to the meeting point, thereby minimizing their own sense of personal responsibility.
Another mechanism is externalizing responsibility: viewing those deviant urges as a separate world detached from their true, real-life self.
A third mechanism is minimizing harm in perception: deliberately avoiding thoughts about the victim's pain or the loss suffered by his family in Hung Yen.
Thanks to these walls of fabricated reasoning, they can eat well, sleep soundly, and maintain an unusually calm demeanor before colleagues for nearly a year.
Another point behavioral psychologists pay close attention to in double-life offenders is the split between cognitive intelligence and emotional morality.
They are fully lucid enough to understand that their actions violate serious laws and are condemned by society.
The clearest proof of that lucidity is the entire sequence of calculating the pre-Tet timing, choosing an empty location, and scrubbing traces immediately afterward.
They did not lose cognitive capacity or behavioral control while executing their plan.
What is missing here is not intelligence or legal awareness, but an internal moral brake system and basic compassion for fellow human beings.
When a deviant ego places personal gratification above another person's life, all social norms are relegated to secondary importance.
Therefore, their calm exterior over the following ten months is not a sign of innocence, but the manifestation of a chillingly cold psychological defense structure."

**INVESTIGATOR** *(adding professional perspective on post-crime psychology)*:

**HOST** *(summarizing the depth of Act 3)*:
