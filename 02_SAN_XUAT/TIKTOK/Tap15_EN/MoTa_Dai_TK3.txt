HISTORICAL BACKGROUND & BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS
——————
Forensic clinical psychiatric evaluations confirmed Jodi Arias suffered from Borderline Personality Disorder (BPD) compounded by malignant narcissistic traits. The hallmark of BPD is an overwhelming terror of real or perceived abandonment, mediated by the cognitive defense mechanism of 'Splitting'—idolizing a partner as divine perfection one moment, then vilifying them as pure evil upon facing rejection. Their intense trauma bonding created an addictive push-pull cycle that ultimately culminated in lethal narcissistic rage.
