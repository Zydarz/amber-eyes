HISTORICAL BACKGROUND & BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS
——————
To execute her deadly trek to Arizona, Jodi Arias executed an elaborate concealment scheme: renting a car in Redding, California, acquiring three spare gas cans to cross thousands of desert miles without leaving a single credit card transaction in Arizona. She dyed her hair dark brown, kept her mobile phone powered off to defeat cell tower pings, and immediately sped to Utah to meet another romantic partner with cheerful smiles, leaving sweet voicemails on Travis's phone to fabricate an ironclad alibi.
