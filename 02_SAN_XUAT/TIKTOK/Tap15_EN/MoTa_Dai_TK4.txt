HISTORICAL BACKGROUND & BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS
——————
Throughout the investigation and sensational 2013 trial, Jodi Arias repeatedly morphed her narratives: initially denying presence in Arizona, then inventing two fictitious masked intruders, and ultimately claiming battered self-defense when physical evidence left no escape. Left alone in interrogation, she sang and performed headstands before CCTV cameras. Her dramatic courtroom weeping embodied clinical 'pathological self-pity' as her omnipotent facade collapsed, wholly devoid of authentic remorse for the lost life.
