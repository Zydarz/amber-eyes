HISTORICAL BACKGROUND & BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS
——————
At the Mesa, Arizona crime scene in summer 2008, the perpetrator attempted an aggressive cleanup to eliminate evidence. The most bizarre discovery was inside the laundry room: a Sony Cyber-shot digital camera submerged beneath water in an active washing machine cycle. Forensic digital analysts successfully recovered all manually deleted photos: the final picture of Travis alive at 5:29 PM in the shower, followed minutes later by accidental chaotic frames capturing Jodi Arias herself, completely ruling out third-party intruders.
