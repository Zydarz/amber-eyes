Psychological Markers: #6 · #7 — Technological Vulnerability in Automated Dispensing & The Angel of Death Escalation.

By the early 2000s, hospitals adopted Pyxis MedStation automated dispensing cabinets to regulate pharmaceuticals. However, Charles Cullen discovered a critical software vulnerability: he entered requests for Digoxin, Insulin, or Norepinephrine, and the moment the physical drawer released, he pressed cancel. The terminal logged the transaction as canceled, yet the lethal vial was already in his pocket.

At Somerset Medical Center, his inhibition collapsed entirely. His psychological signature—the unchanging emotional gratification compelling his crimes—was a profound "God Complex," imagining himself as the sole arbiter of who survived.
