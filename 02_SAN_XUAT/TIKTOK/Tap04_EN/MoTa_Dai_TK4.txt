Psychological Markers: #9 · #3 — Institutional Bystander Effect & 400 Victims in the Shadows.

Somerset Medical Center was not his initial crime scene. From 1988 to 2003, Charles Cullen worked across 9 hospitals and 1 nursing home spanning New Jersey and Pennsylvania. Wherever he worked, nighttime cardiac arrest fatalities spiked along an anomalous statistical curve.

Whenever suspicion mounted, he was quietly forced out with neutral references, seamlessly transferring to another facility. Analysts believe the true toll could reach 400 lives. In 2004, New Jersey enacted the landmark Cullen Act to permanently dismantle dangerous institutional silence.
