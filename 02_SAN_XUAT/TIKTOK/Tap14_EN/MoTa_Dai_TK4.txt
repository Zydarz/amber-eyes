HISTORICAL BACKGROUND & BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS
——————
At dawn on November 5, 1989, using a key stolen from her daughter, Betty infiltrated Dan and Linda's bedroom and fired five revolver shots, killing both in their sleep. In court, eldest daughter Kim Broderick's testimony exposed premeditation. Handed a 32-year-to-life sentence, Betty remains entrenched in perpetual victimhood to this day, exhibiting zero genuine remorse.
