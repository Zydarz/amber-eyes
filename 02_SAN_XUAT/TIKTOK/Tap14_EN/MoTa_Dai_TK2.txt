HISTORICAL BACKGROUND & BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS
——————
In autumn 1983, Dan hired 21-year-old Linda Kolkena, sparking Betty's sharp marital intuition. Instead of honesty, Dan weaponized supreme courtroom eloquence to conduct a 2-year gaslighting campaign, insisting Betty was delusional and mentally unstable. This deliberate reality denial obliterated her cognitive grounding, transforming betrayal trauma into an uncontrollable psychological tempest of rage.
