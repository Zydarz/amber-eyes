HISTORICAL BACKGROUND & BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS
——————
For 16 arduous years, Betty Broderick worked multiple blue-collar jobs—teaching preschool, selling Tupperware, and marketing Avon—to single-handedly finance her husband Daniel's dual degrees: Cornell Medicine and Harvard Law. Yet when Dan ascended to multimillionaire status as San Diego Bar Association President, their opulent La Jolla lifestyle morphed into the ultimate sunk-cost trap of her life.
