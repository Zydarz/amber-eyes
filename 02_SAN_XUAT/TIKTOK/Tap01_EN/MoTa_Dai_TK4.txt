MARKER #10: 4 SURVIVAL RED FLAGS (MEDICAL CHILD ABUSE)
——————
How do we recognize when a child is being systematically fabricated into an invalid within their own household? Medical and psychological consensus identifies four critical red flags:

① COMPLEX MEDICAL HISTORY CONTRADICTING OBJECTIVE LABS: An endless litany of catastrophic conditions (leukemia, muscular dystrophy, seizures), yet all independent diagnostic scans and blood panels return completely normal or directly contradict the caregiver's narrative.
② SYMPTOMS RESTRICTED TO CAREGIVER PRESENCE: Manifestations appear solely when the caregiver is present, resolving promptly when the patient is admitted to independent monitored care.
③ CHRONIC DOCTOR SHOPPING & ATTENTION SEEKING: Abruptly abandoning specialists the moment hard questions arise, coupled with aggressive oversharing on social media to farm public sympathy and financial gifts.
④ COMPLETE SOCIAL ISOLATION: The child is systematically severed from schooling, peers, and extended family under the righteous guise of protecting compromised immunity.
