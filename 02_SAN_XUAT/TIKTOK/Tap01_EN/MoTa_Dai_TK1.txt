MARKER #7: MODUS OPERANDI VS PSYCHOLOGICAL SIGNATURE
——————
When the search for Gypsy Rose Blanchard ended after 48 hours, the public expected to find a helpless invalid abducted by a murderer. Instead, she walked through the doors of the interrogation room on her own two feet, completely unassisted.

This pivotal revelation splits criminal behavior into two distinct concepts:
• MODUS OPERANDI (MO): The dynamic methods used to commit the offense and prevent detection. Dee Dee Blanchard continuously adapted her MO: changing doctors, altering clinics, and relocating across states whenever an astute physician grew suspicious.
• PSYCHOLOGICAL SIGNATURE: The static, immutable emotional core of the crime. Dee Dee's signature was the systematic theft of adulthood: altering birth certificates to freeze her 20-year-old daughter into a perpetual 16-year-old dependent child.

She did not merely want a sick child; she compulsively needed a child who would never grow up.

——————
CASE DOSSIER CONTEXT
——————
In June 2015, Clauddinnea "Dee Dee" Blanchard was found dead in her Springfield, Missouri home. For over two decades, the community revered Dee Dee as the ultimate devoted mother. The subsequent trial unveiled one of the most severe documented cases of Factitious Disorder Imposed on Another (FDIA / Munchausen by Proxy).
