MARKERS #3 & #9: NARCISSISTIC SUPPLY & SOCIAL BLIND SPOTS
——————
Many assume Dee Dee Blanchard fabricated her daughter's illnesses merely for disability checks and complimentary Disney trips. Clinical profiling reveals a starkly different truth:
• MALINGERING vs FDIA: A malingerer fakes symptoms for tangible material gain, quietly exploiting loopholes in the shadows. Factitious Disorder Imposed on Another (FDIA), however, pursues an intoxicating emotional narcotic: narcissistic supply.
• THE HEROIC MOTHER ARTIFACT: After a life of compounding failures and isolation, Dee Dee transformed into the revered saint of Springfield. Every accolade praising her extraordinary sacrifice provided a massive hit of dopamine, driving an insatiable addiction.
• COLLECTIVE SOCIAL BLIND SPOT: Society expects evil to look grotesque; when evil dresses in pastel sweaters, charitable smiles, and sacrificial devotion, collective critical thinking completely evaporates.
