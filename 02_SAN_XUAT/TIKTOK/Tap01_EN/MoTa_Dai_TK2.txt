MARKER #6: LEARNED HELPLESSNESS & TRAUMA BONDING
——————
For decades, surrounded by hundreds of physicians, specialists, and law enforcement officers, Gypsy Rose never once leaped out of her wheelchair to shout for salvation.

To understand why a victim remains trapped in plain sight, behavioral psychology identifies two critical cognitive cages:
• LEARNED HELPLESSNESS (Seligman, 1967): When subjected to continuous unavoidable pain and control, repeated failures to escape condition the organism to extinguish all resistance. Even when the doors are opened, the traumatized mind firmly concludes that any resistance is utterly futile.
• COVERT COERCIVE CONTROL: Dee Dee gripped Gypsy's hand during every medical consultation. Observers viewed it as maternal tenderness; to the daughter, it was a silent threat that any misstep would result in physical punishment once the clinic doors shut.
