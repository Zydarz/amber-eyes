HISTORICAL BACKGROUND & BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS
——————
The Jonestown tragedy clinically illustrates Stanley Milgram's Obedience to Authority and Irving Janis's Groupthink. When facing perceived legitimate authority, individuals transfer moral agency to the commander. Internal group pressure crushed dissenting voices, creating massive cognitive blind spots. Through "Foot-in-the-Door" escalating commitment, followers surrendered autonomy incrementally until trapped in blind compliance.
