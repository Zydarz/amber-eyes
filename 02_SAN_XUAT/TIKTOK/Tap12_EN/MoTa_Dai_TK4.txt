HISTORICAL BACKGROUND & BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS
——————
The FBI declassified cassette tape Q 042 (44 minutes) captures the raw audio of November 18, 1978. Amidst overwhelming conformity, 60-year-old Christine Miller boldly stepped to the microphone to confront Jim Jones directly: "I feel that as long as there is life, there is hope." Her defiance embodies independent moral conscience, standing in stark contrast to Jones's narcissistic neutralization and externalization of blame.
