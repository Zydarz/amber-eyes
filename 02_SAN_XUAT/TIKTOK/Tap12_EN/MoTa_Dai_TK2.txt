HISTORICAL BACKGROUND & BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS
——————
In the remote jungle of Guyana, Jonestown residents were subjected to absolute isolation. Passports and money were confiscated at entry. Devotees endured 11 hours of forced daily labor under brutal tropical heat on meager rations, surrounded by armed guards. Weekly "Catharsis sessions" forced members into public humiliation and mutual denunciation, shattering all familial ties and creating total dependence on Jones.
