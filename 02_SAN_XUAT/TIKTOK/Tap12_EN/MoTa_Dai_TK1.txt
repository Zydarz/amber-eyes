HISTORICAL BACKGROUND & BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS
——————
Jim Jones founded the Peoples Temple, presenting it as a progressive beacon of racial harmony and social justice. Adopting children of diverse races as his "Rainbow Family", he operated free dining halls and drug rehab centers in San Francisco. This flawless moral facade earned him the chairmanship of the San Francisco Housing Authority. The resulting Halo Effect and political blind spots completely paralyzed law enforcement oversight for years.
