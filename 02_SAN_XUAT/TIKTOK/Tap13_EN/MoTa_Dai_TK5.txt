HISTORICAL BACKGROUND & BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS
——————
Drawing from the Jennifer Pan tragedy, Amber Eyes highlights 4 vital familial red flags: (1) Conditional love tied strictly to high achievement and fear of failure; (2) Compounding webs of deceit and prolonged double lives; (3) Excessive authoritarian control crushing autonomy; (4) Toxic silence and emotional rupture under the same roof. A genuine home must be the safest sanctuary to fall when failing, not an inescapable courtroom of judgment.
