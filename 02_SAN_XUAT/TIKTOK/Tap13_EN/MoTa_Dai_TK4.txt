HISTORICAL BACKGROUND & BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS
——————
The case pivoted dramatically when father Huei Hann Pan miraculously awoke from his coma. His testimony shattered Jennifer's alibi: he recalled seeing Jennifer walking freely through the house, whispering cordially with the armed intruders, completely unbound. On November 22, 2010, Detective William Goetz confronted her in an 8-hour psychological interrogation with forensic cellphone evidence, systematically dismantling her defense.
