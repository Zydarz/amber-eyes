HISTORICAL BACKGROUND & BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS
——————
To neighbors in Markham, Ontario, Jennifer Pan was the epitome of the 'golden child'—polite, decorated in figure skating, and an accomplished pianist. Yet beneath this flawless facade lay an invisible domestic cage forged by extreme 'Tiger Parenting'. Her father strictly forbade dating, parties, makeup, and social outings. The relentless pressure and terror of failing parental expectations quietly eroded her psychological well-being throughout her adolescence.
