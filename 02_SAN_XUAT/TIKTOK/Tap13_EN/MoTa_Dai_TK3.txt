HISTORICAL BACKGROUND & BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS
——————
On November 8, 2010, a violent armed home invasion occurred at 238 Helen Avenue, leaving her mother deceased and her father critically wounded. Jennifer dialed 911 in hysterical panic. Yet detectives immediately noticed glaring anomalies: no signs of forced entry, valuables left untouched, and most bafflingly, Jennifer claimed her hands were bound tightly behind her back to the banister, yet managed to retrieve and dial her mobile phone flawlessly.
