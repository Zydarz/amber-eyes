HISTORICAL BACKGROUND & BEHAVIORAL ANALYSIS
——————
When failing high school calculus threatened to destroy her father's pride, Jennifer used razor blades and a photocopier to forge report cards. That first successful lie initiated a decade-long house of cards. She pretended to attend the University of Toronto's prestigious pharmacy program, commuting every morning only to sit in public libraries reading novels. The deception escalated into forged university diplomas and fabricated research jobs at SickKids Hospital.
