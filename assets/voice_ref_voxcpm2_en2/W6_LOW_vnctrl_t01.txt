In the eyes of all of us at Trenton base, Colonel Williams was the very picture of perfection. He was calm, correct down to every crease of his uniform, worked tirelessly, and never once raised his voice at a subordinate. But after everything came to light, whenever we recall his placid gaze at the parades, we all feel a chill. We had stood beside a most dangerous predator, and still bowed our heads in admiration.
